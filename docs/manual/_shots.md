## 1.4.0: what must be reshot

House rule: never reuse an image when its page or dialog changed; reuse is fine when nothing on it changed. Every
shot of the **main window** shows the sidebar's version (**Version 1.3.0**, now **Version 1.4.0**), so all of them
are reshot on 1.4.0. Take **every** shot with **no SHOBS-FLUX profile** on the computer (an empty or missing
`%USERPROFILE%\.shobs` folder) and nothing typed in Setup's Photometry noise box, so the verdicts read *Detectable?*
and so on, transits are on Longest safe, and the numbers match chapter 5's worked example. Use the example site:
Example Observatory, latitude 40.0, longitude −100.0, elevation 0, America/Chicago; telescope 250 mm aperture, 1600 mm
focal length, 3.76 µm pixels, binning 1, camera ZWO ASI2600MC Air (Color), no gain setting or e⁻ per ADU, linear
limit 32000.
